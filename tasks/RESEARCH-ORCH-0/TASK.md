# RESEARCH-ORCH-0: how does "one coordinator, many agents" work best? Learn from existing products and projects
Our topology: super (human) → per project: Leader ∥ Auditor (peers, senior, possibly expensive models, TUI) → many workers (cheap, mostly headless). Design target: workers carry ~90% of spend; observed in our experiments: Leader+Auditor 25–40%. Open questions: right coordinator:worker ratio, how/when the coordinator is woken, how results flow back (push vs pull, summaries vs raw), whether a separate auditor/reviewer helps or hurts (interrupting the leader, asking for reports), cost split, failure modes.
